About

I trained as a geographer and have spent my career where maps meet software.

My work brings together careful cartography, from detailed nautical-style charts to interactive web maps, and the data behind it: cleaning messy public datasets, building repeatable pipelines, and turning demographic and market data into answers people can act on. I work with open-source tools, taking raw government data all the way to finished print and web maps.

I'm available for freelance mapping, data extraction and spatial analysis work.
